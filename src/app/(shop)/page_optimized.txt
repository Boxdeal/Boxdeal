OPTIMIZATION SUMMARY FOR HOME PAGE:

Current Issues:
- Initial 6 queries (fast - parallel)
- Then for EACH category (usually 10+), TWO more queries run:
  - 1 query for category products
  - 1 query for subcategories
  - Then for EACH subcategory (usually 3-5), 1 more query
- Total: 6 + (categories * 2) + (total_subcategories) = 6 + (10*2) + (30) = 56 queries!

OPTIMIZATION PLAN (Reduce to ~12 queries):
1. Keep initial 6 queries (banners, categories, brands, deals, featured)
2. Instead of fetching all categories with products:
   - Show only TOP 3-4 categories in sections
   - Limit subcategories per category to 2
   - Batch fetch all section products in one/two queries

Result: 6 + (4 categories * 2) + (8 subcats) = ~22 queries (60% reduction)

ALTERNATIVE (BEST - Reduce to ~10 queries):
1. Keep initial 6 queries
2. Fetch categories with subcategories in one RPC call or with limits
3. Only load visible sections (top 3 categories)
4. Load remaining sections on-demand with lazy loading

This would reduce to ~10 queries total (82% reduction)
